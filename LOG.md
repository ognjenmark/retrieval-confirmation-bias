**Design fixed before any result for it.**
- Model: Qwen3-4B, `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-GGUF`, file `Qwen3-4B-Q4_K_M.gguf`, revision `bc640142c66e1fdd12af0bd68f40445458f3869b`, through `llama-cpp-python` on CPU, thinking disabled (`/no_think`), temperature 0.
- q_wrong: the model answers each question closed-book with three guesses, most likely first. The first guess that passes the same string check as q_random (no overlap with the correct answer, not in a gold document) is the wrong candidate; questions without one are dropped and counted. A guess that matches the correct answer is counted as a correct closed-book guess. q_wrong is q_keywords plus that candidate.
- Reader (H4): for each question the model sees the top 5 corpus documents of a query, each cut to the 200-word window with the most query terms, and judges a proposed answer as SUPPORTED, REFUTED or UNCLEAR. Four conditions per question: wrong candidate with q_keywords results and with q_wrong results; correct answer with q_keywords results and with q_true results. The main H4 number is the paired difference in the share of SUPPORTED for the wrong candidate, q_wrong results minus q_keywords results. A gold document in the top 5 is used as an automatic proxy for refuting evidence until the manual labels exist.
